👥 HR Analytics Dashboard</br>

About the Project</br>

This Power BI project analyzes employee attrition by combining workforce demographics, salary, education, job roles, tenure, and satisfaction metrics.</br>
The dashboard helps identify turnover patterns and highlights factors that influence employee retention across different departments and employee groups.</br>

Key Findings</br>

The organization recorded an overall attrition rate of 16.1%.</br>
Employees aged 26–35 experienced the highest turnover.</br>
Lower salary bands showed significantly higher attrition.</br>
Sales Representatives recorded the highest employee exits.</br>
Life Sciences contributed the largest share of attrition by education background.</br>
Employee turnover was highest during the initial years of employment.</br>

Project Outcome</br>

The report helps HR professionals understand workforce trends, identify high-risk employee groups, evaluate retention challenges, and support strategic decisions related to recruitment, employee engagement, and talent retention.</br>
